Problem Statement & Real-World Need

Traditional campus placement management relies on fragmented communication channels (emails, notice boards, manual spreadsheets), causing several critical issues:

  • Lack of Real-time Updates: Students miss crucial drive deadlines or schedule changes.
  • Eligibility Tracking Issues: Manual verification of CGPA and backlogs is prone to errors.
  • Data Redundancy: Repeated student registration for individual visiting companies.

Proposed Core Solution

A dynamic web portal bridging placement officers, recruiters, and graduating students through streamlined features:

  • Unified Registration: Single-window application with dynamic jQuery validation.
  • AJAX & JSON/XML Integration: Live asynchronous filtering of company data.
  • AngularJS Data Binding: Real-time statistics, search filtering, and state management.
